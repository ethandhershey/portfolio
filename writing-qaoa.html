Reflection

This report documents my implementation and analysis of the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm applied to an EV charging scheduling problem. It covers classical optimization, QUBO conversion, circuit simulation at different depths, and noisy hardware modeling using FakeKolkataV2. One of the harder parts of writing this was explaining why certain design decisions were made under real hardware constraints, not just what the results were. Technical reports that only describe outputs without justifying choices are not useful. Writing this forced me to be precise about tradeoffs, which is something I try to carry into all technical work. For an employer, it shows I can handle genuinely complex technical problems and communicate the results clearly.
