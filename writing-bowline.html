Reflection

This instruction set walks a beginner through tying a bowline knot in three phases. The challenge was writing steps that are specific enough to follow without being so detailed they become hard to read. I learned how much word choice matters in procedural writing: one ambiguous verb and the reader does the wrong thing. I also had to think carefully about where warnings belong in the sequence, since a caution notice is only useful if it appears before the mistake can happen. For an employer, this shows I can write clear, user-centered documentation. That translates directly to writing runbooks, onboarding guides, API docs, and any other procedural material that engineers are regularly expected to produce.
