Reflection

This document describes the principal parts of a mechanical keyboard for a general audience. Writing it taught me how to take something I already knew well and explain it clearly to someone with no background in it. Stripping out jargon and leading with what actually matters to the reader is harder than it sounds. The bigger challenge was organizing information by part rather than by how I personally think about the product, which forced me to think from the reader's perspective. For an employer, this shows I can communicate technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders, something that comes up constantly in engineering work when writing specs, documentation, or client-facing materials.
